Drafty

Privacy Policy

Drafty is built for live, temporary conversations. This policy explains what information is used to operate the service and how it is handled.

1. Information we use

Drafty may process the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information, such as your email address, display name, username or other account details you provide.
  • Invitation and connection information, including email invitations you choose to send and your accepted Drafty connections.
  • Buzz information, such as sender, recipient, status, timestamps and expiry information needed to deliver and manage a Buzz.
  • Push notification information, including the device token required to send native notifications to your device.
  • Safety information, including blocks, reports and related information needed to operate safety and abuse-prevention features.
  • Basic technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. Live Draft conversations

Drafty is designed around temporary, one-to-one live Draft sessions. Draft content is used to deliver the live session between participants.

When a Draft ends, Drafty is designed not to keep a permanent chat transcript for you to return to later.

Drafty may process session data temporarily while a Draft is active and for the limited technical operations required to provide, secure and troubleshoot the service.

3. Invitations and contacts

Drafty does not upload your address book or contacts list. Invitations are sent only when you choose to enter an email address and send an invitation.

Drafty is invitation-based. Other users cannot freely discover your private contact list through the app.

4. Buzz notifications

A Buzz is a short-lived invitation to start a live Draft. Buzz notifications do not contain Draft message text. Push notifications may include limited technical identifiers required to open the correct Buzz flow in the app.

5. Screenshots and screen recording

Drafty may use iOS-provided signals to detect certain screenshot or screen-recording activity and may surface an alert inside the experience. These features are intended to support user awareness; they do not guarantee that content cannot be copied or captured by another person or device.

6. Why we use information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To send invitations and manage your Drafty connections.
  • To deliver Buzz requests and push notifications.
  • To run live Draft sessions.
  • To provide blocking, reporting and account-safety features.
  • To prevent abuse, maintain security and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Service providers

Drafty relies on service providers to operate parts of the app, such as cloud infrastructure, authentication, database services, email delivery and Apple Push Notification service. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to Drafty and are subject to their own legal and security obligations.

8. Data retention

Account and operational information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

Live Draft conversations are designed to be temporary rather than stored as a permanent user-accessible chat history.

9. Your choices

You can use Drafty’s in-app controls to manage connections, block or report users, and delete your account where available. You may also contact us regarding privacy questions or requests.

10. Children

Drafty is not specifically designed as a service for children. If additional age restrictions are introduced for the service, this policy and the App Store listing will be updated accordingly.

11. Security

Drafty uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and service information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Drafty evolves. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
