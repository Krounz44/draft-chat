Inviting a Drafty

Drafty is invitation-based. Use the invite option in the app and enter the private email address the person uses for Drafty. Drafty does not upload your contacts.

Buzz

A Buzz is a short-lived invitation to start a live Draft. It contains no message text and expires automatically if it is not answered.

Live Drafts

Drafts are one-to-one live sessions. Only one person writes at a time. New content replaces what came before, and the session is designed to disappear when it ends rather than remain as a permanent chat transcript.

Notifications

If Buzz notifications are not arriving, check iPhone or iPad Settings → Notifications → Drafty and make sure notifications are allowed.

Blocking and reporting

Drafty includes controls to block and report users. Use the relevant in-app options if you no longer want to interact with someone or need to report inappropriate behavior.

Account deletion

If you want to delete your Drafty account, use the account deletion option available in the app. If you cannot access your account, contact support using the email above.

Privacy

Read the Drafty Privacy Policy at draft-chat.com/privacy.
